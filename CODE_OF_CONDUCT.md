# Code of conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our standard

- Be respectful and patient with other people.
- Give and accept feedback on the work, not on the person.
- Do not harass, insult or discriminate.
- Do not publish the private information of other people.

## Enforcement

Maintainers remove comments, issues and pull requests that break this standard.
They can ban a person from the project for repeated or serious breaks.
Report a problem by email to [vexzyl@pm.me](mailto:vexzyl@pm.me).
Maintainers keep the identity of the reporter private.
